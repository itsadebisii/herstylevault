What to Expect: Styling PackagesYour styling journey, explained.

Every HerStyleVault package is built around multiple sessions so nothing is rushed and every detail is right. Here's exactly what each session looks like, and how to prepare.

Please Keep In Mind
"Great style takes time, and you deserve that time."
Sessions are spaced at least 1 week apart intentionally. This gives me time to research, source, and build your looks properly between each touchpoint. Your first session also sits at least 1 week out from the day you book. Please book with your timeline in mind. The earlier you start the process, the better your results.
01Free consultation

A 30-minute call to find the right fit.

02Your sessions

We build your outfits together, a week apart.

03Style Snapshot

A quick visual of your color type, persona, body shape and palette lands in your Style Hub after Session 1.

04Your Style Guide

Your full Style Analysis and every outfit, 1 to 2 weeks after your final session.
