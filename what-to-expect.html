What to Expect

Your styling journey, explained.

Every HerStyleVault package is built around multiple sessions so nothing is rushed and every detail is right. Here's exactly what each session looks like — and how to prepare.

Please Keep In Mind
"Great style takes time — and you deserve that time."
Sessions are spaced at least 1 week apart intentionally. This gives me time to research, source, and build your looks properly between each touchpoint. Please book with your timeline in mind — the earlier you start the process, the better your results.
