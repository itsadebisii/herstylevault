Personal Styling FAQYour questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before booking your session with HerStyleVault.

Do I need a lot of clothes to book a session?+
Not at all! Whether you have a full closet or just a few pieces, I work with what you have. Before your first outfit session, send photos of the pieces you already love or want worked into your outfits, and I'll build around them where they fit and identify exactly what's missing. A full closet audit is part of The Full Reveal.
Do you style all body types?+
Absolutely yes. HerStyleVault is for every woman regardless of size, shape, or body type. My approach is always body-positive and focused on what makes you feel confident and like your best self.
Is the consultation really free?+
Yes, completely free and no commitment required. The free consultation is for first-time clients only: a 30-minute call for us to connect, discuss your style goals, and find the right package or service for you. Returning clients don't need one and can book directly.
What if I have a Pinterest board or vision board?+
Please share it! You can paste your Pinterest board link directly in the intake form or email any inspiration images to hello@herstylevault.com.
Are The Unlock and The Vault Edit in person or virtual?+
Either. Virtual sessions use Google Meet or Zoom. In person means we meet face to face and I can work with the pieces you own to build your outfits. A full closet audit and three shopping trips are part of The Full Reveal, which is fully in person. One guided shopping trip can be added to The Unlock or The Vault Edit. Travel is free within 1 hour of Boston or Southern NH, and $25 per in-person session for 1 to 2.5 hours.
Which package includes the in-person shopping trip?+
Three in-person shopping trips are included in The Full Reveal. The Unlock and The Vault Edit include a personalized shopping list with sourced links, and you can add one Guided Shopping Trip for $100 (up to 2 hours, in person, retail or thrift). Travel fees apply.
How much should I budget for new clothes?+
There's no set minimum. We discuss your clothing budget during the consultation call and I build your shopping recommendations around whatever feels comfortable for you.
How many sessions are included in each package?+
The Unlock includes 3 sessions, The Vault Edit includes 4 sessions, and The Full Reveal includes 5 sessions, plus a free consultation for first-time clients. Additional sessions are available for $50/hr.
What's the difference between À La Carte Services and Private Styling Packages?+
À La Carte Services (Occasion Edit, The Ensemble, The Mini Collection, The Wardrobe Detox) are one-off bookings open to anyone, no consultation or package required. Private Styling Packages are multi-session engagements that include a color and body analysis, a shopping list with links, and a personalized style guide.
When is final payment due?+
Full payment must be received before your final session. Nothing is delivered until payment is complete.
How long until I receive my style guide?+
Your personalized style guide and outfit lookbook will be delivered within 1–2 weeks after your final session.
Do you offer refunds?+
All sales are final. If you have any concerns please reach out before booking and I'm happy to answer your questions. The Policies page has the full details for deposits, services, and digital products.
Is my session information kept private?+
Absolutely. Your session details, personal information, and photos are never shared publicly without your explicit written permission.
Do you style men?+
I don't offer full packages for men, but I do take occasional one-off looks, like an Occasion Edit for a wedding, date night or event. Email me at hello@herstylevault.com with what's coming up and I'll let you know if I can fit you in. And if you're shopping for someone you love, Style-11 is free: answer a few questions and it finds her an outfit.
