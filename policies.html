Free Consultation

Tap to reveal
The free 30-minute consultation call is for first-time clients only. Every new client begins with one. No obligation, no payment required. On the call I'll recommend whichever fits you best: a Private Styling Package or an À La Carte Service. Returning clients don't need a consultation and can book directly.
  • Book your consultation through the Calendly link provided after your intake form is submitted
  • Please complete your intake form before the consultation so I can review your information beforehand
  • Share your Pinterest board or any style inspiration in your intake form or via email before the call

À La Carte Services Availability

Tap to reveal
Occasion Edit, The Ensemble, The Mini Collection, and The Wardrobe Detox are standalone services — no consultation or package required, open to new and returning clients alike.
  • Full payment required before work begins
  • Occasion Edit: 3-5 day turnaround (Quick Fit) or 2 week lead time (Standard/Max)
  • The Ensemble: 2 week lead time
  • The Mini Collection: 3 week lead time (Standard) or 4 week lead time (Max)
  • The Wardrobe Detox: book at least 1 week ahead
  • Lead times start once your form and payment are received
  • Occasion Edit, The Ensemble & The Mini Collection: delivered by email as a finished PDF
  • The Wardrobe Detox: in person, half day or full day, scheduled directly

Sessions & Scheduling

Tap to reveal
  • The Unlock: 3 sessions total
  • The Vault Edit: 4 sessions total
  • The Full Reveal: 5 sessions total, all in person (Session 1: closet audit at your home, Sessions 2-4: shopping trips, Session 5: The Reveal)
  • The Unlock and The Vault Edit sessions can be in person or virtual. In person, I can work with the pieces you own to build your outfits; a full closet audit is exclusive to The Full Reveal, and one Guided Shopping Trip (in person, up to 2 hours, $100) can be added to The Unlock or The Vault Edit
  • Your first session is scheduled at least 1 week after you book
  • Sessions are scheduled at least 1 week apart minimum
  • All sessions must be completed within 60 days of your deposit for The Unlock and The Vault Edit, or 90 days for The Full Reveal — life happens, so reach out if you need more time and we'll figure it out together
  • You'll book each next session directly with me after your current one wraps — sessions aren't all scheduled upfront
  • Additional sessions available for $50/hr for all packages

Deposits & Payment

Tap to reveal
⚠️ First-time clients: after your consultation I'll email you your first-session booking link, your 50% deposit Wave invoice, and your client agreement to sign. Your deposit is due within 48 hours of booking your first session to secure your session dates. If it isn't received, your dates will be released and the booking cancelled. Returning clients starting a new package: your 50% deposit is paid by card directly in the booking form. (Continuing a package you're already in? Your deposit is already paid.)
  • If you wish to rebook after a missed deposit deadline the full deposit is required upfront — no exceptions
  • Full payment must be received before your final session — nothing is delivered without complete payment
  • Payment methods: returning-client deposits are paid by card in the booking form; first-time deposits and all final balances are sent as a Wave invoice
  • Clients are responsible for all clothing purchases — HerStyleVault sources and curates but does not purchase items on your behalf, and in-person shopping trips are paid directly to each retailer

Refund Policy

Tap to reveal
  • All sales are final — deposits are non-refundable under all circumstances. When you cancel with more than 48 hours' notice, your deposit becomes a credit, not a refund (see Cancellations & Rescheduling)
  • No refunds will be issued after your styling session has taken place
  • À La Carte Services are paid in full before work begins. To change your details or cancel, email hello@herstylevault.com within 24 hours of booking and before I've started your service. A cancellation becomes a credit toward a future service. Once work has started, payment is non-refundable and changes are booked as a new service. The Wardrobe Detox follows the cancellation rule below
  • If you have concerns about a deliverable, whether a style guide or a service PDF, please email hello@herstylevault.com within 48 hours of receiving it
  • All Vault Digitals are final sale because they're delivered instantly. If a file doesn't arrive or won't open, email me and I'll fix it

Travel Policy

Tap to reveal
  • Free travel: Within 1 hour — Northern MA, Greater Boston, and Southern NH
  • Travel fee: +$25 per in-person session for distances between 1–2.5 hours
  • Travel fees apply to in-person sessions, in-person shopping trips, and The Wardrobe Detox
  • Beyond 2.5 hours: case by case — email hello@herstylevault.com before booking (first-time clients can also ask during the consultation). When your location is more than 2.5 hours away, your travel fee and payment are sent as a Wave invoice instead of being paid in the booking form
  • Travel fees are charged per in-person session, added to your invoice, and paid together with your deposit. For in-person À La Carte Services, your travel fee is shown and added before you book

Cancellations & Rescheduling

Tap to reveal
  • Cancel at least 48 hours before your session (more than 48 hours' notice): your deposit can be applied as a credit to a future session within 30 days (credit, not a refund)
  • Cancel less than 48 hours before your session: your deposit is forfeited
  • The Wardrobe Detox: cancel at least 48 hours before your scheduled time (more than 48 hours' notice) and it can be rescheduled or credited; cancel less than 48 hours before and your payment is forfeited
  • Rescheduling requests must be made at least 48 hours in advance
  • HerStyleVault reserves the right to reschedule due to emergencies with at least 24 hours notice

Missed Sessions

Tap to reveal
⚠️ Sessions missed without any prior contact will result in a charge.
  • 1st missed session with no contact: $15 charge
  • 2nd missed session with no contact: $30 charge
  • 3rd missed session and every missed session after that: $50 charge each
  • These fees are added to your remaining balance and must be paid before your next session or delivery

Privacy & Confidentiality

Tap to reveal
  • Your session details, personal information, and photos are never shared publicly without your explicit written permission
  • Before and after photos used for portfolio purposes will only be posted with your written consent
  • Your information is never sold, and never shared with anyone other than the tools below that I use to deliver your service
  • I use a small set of trusted tools to run HerStyleVault: Tally (forms), Calendly (scheduling), Stripe (card payments), Wave (invoices), Notion (your Style Hub), and Google Meet or Zoom (calls). They handle your information only to provide their service
  • The Signature Style Report uses an AI service to help write your personal note. Your quiz answers, and your photo if you choose to upload one, are sent to it only to generate your report. HerStyleVault does not store your photo
