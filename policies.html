Deposits & Payment

Tap to reveal

  • You pay for your own clothes. I find and choose pieces, but I never buy anything for you. On shopping trips, you pay each store yourself.

Cancelling & Rescheduling

Tap to reveal

Changing one session

  • Need a new time? Tell me at least 48 hours before your session. It's free. We'll pick a new time inside your package window.
  • Less than 48 hours? Each package gets one free "life happens" pass. After that, each late change costs $25.
  • Real emergencies, like illness or a family emergency: tell me. I'll work with you.
  • If I have an emergency, I'll tell you at least 24 hours ahead and move your session for free.
  • Your package window is 60 days for The Unlock and The Vault Edit, and 90 days for The Full Reveal. All sessions need to fit inside it.

Cancelling before you start

  • Cancel before your first session with more than 48 hours' notice: your deposit is saved as credit for 30 days. You can use it on a new booking in that time. It is not refunded, and it expires after 30 days.
  • Cancel with less than 48 hours' notice: you lose your deposit.

Stopping after you've started

  • Your deposit is not refunded.
  • If you've finished more than half of your sessions, the rest of your balance is still due. That means 2 of 3 sessions for The Unlock, 3 of 4 for The Vault Edit, and 3 of 5 for The Full Reveal.
  • If you've finished half or fewer, you owe nothing more. You just don't get your deposit back.
  • Sessions you didn't use end when your package window ends.
  • You keep everything I've already shared with you in your Style Hub, like your Style Snapshot and any outfits I've added.
  • If you've paid in full, I'll deliver a shorter style guide with the work we finished. It won't include outfits I never started.
  • If you haven't paid in full, I can't deliver your style guide. If you come back inside your package window and pay the rest, you can finish your sessions and get the full guide.

The Wardrobe Detox

  • Cancel with more than 48 hours' notice: we can pick a new date, or I'll save your payment as credit.
  • Cancel with less than 48 hours' notice: you lose your payment.

Privacy & Confidentiality

Tap to reveal
  • Your session details, personal information, and photos are never shared publicly without your explicit written permission
  • Before and after photos used for portfolio purposes will only be posted with your written consent
  • Your information is never sold, and never shared with anyone other than the tools below that I use to deliver your service
  • I use a small set of trusted tools to run HerStyleVault: Tally (forms), Calendly (scheduling), Stripe (card payments), Wave (invoices), Notion (your Style Hub), Google Workspace (a private sheet of Signature Style Report quiz leads) and Google Meet or Zoom (calls). They handle your information only to provide their service
  • Your finished Signature Style Report is stored privately so you can reopen it online on any device. It opens with the email you used at checkout, or through a link if you choose to share it. A shared link shows your report to anyone who has it, so share it only with people you trust. It never includes your photo, email or age, and you can turn it off any time. Your photo is never stored. Email hello@herstylevault.com any time and I will delete your report
  • The Signature Style Report uses an AI service to help write your personal note. Your quiz answers, and your photo if you choose to upload one, are sent to it only to generate your report. The quiz also asks your first name, email and age bracket. Your name and email are saved when you start the quiz, even if you don't finish, so I can send you your report and help if something goes wrong. I only send marketing emails, such as style tips and offers, if you tick the optional box on the quiz start page. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, or you can email hello@herstylevault.com and I will remove you right away. Report emails are not marketing, so you still get those. Your age bracket is used only to personalize your Style Persona. HerStyleVault does not store your photo
  • Before the Signature Style Report quiz begins, we ask if you might book a styling session in the future. If you say yes, we keep your quiz answers and results on file so you do not have to start over. When you book, we will ask at your consultation or first session whether you want to use that report or run a new analysis, which is worth doing if you feel your answers would be different now. If you say no, we keep only your name and email, and your analysis is done in your session if you book later. You can ask us to delete your saved answers and results at any time by emailing hello@herstylevault.com
