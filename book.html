What Happens After You Book

01

Confirmation

You'll receive a calendar confirmation immediately. I'll follow up within 2–3 business days to confirm all details.

02

Deposit Invoice

After your consultation I'll email your first-session booking link, a 50% deposit invoice, and your client agreement. The deposit is due within 48 hours of booking your first session. Unpaid deposits result in cancellation.

03

Style Hub Access

Once your deposit is confirmed, you'll receive access to your personal Style Hub, a private space where your color analysis, outfit builds, and shopping list live throughout our work together.

04

We Begin

Sessions scheduled, vision locked in, deposit received, and your HerStyleVault experience officially begins. Your first session sits at least 1 week out. Full payment is due before your final session or delivery date.

Booking a service instead? You pay upfront and your PDF is emailed to you. Still deciding? Check the FAQ or Policies before booking.
