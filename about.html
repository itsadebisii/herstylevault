Meet Adebisi

Personal Styling, Rooted in Boston & Southern NH

I'm Adebisi, but you can call me Bisi. I'm a personal stylist and wardrobe consultant, and I started HerStyleVault to share something I've loved for as long as I can remember: putting together a look that actually feels like you.

I serve clients in person around Boston, MA and Southern NH, and virtually wherever you are.

My love for this started years before I ever called it a business. My aunt had me in the cutest little outfits since I was 3, and my mom got my ears pierced when I was only months old. Between the two of them I grew up loving accessories, perfume, shoes, clothes, all of it. I still gravitate toward color and pattern, pieces that stand out a little (maybe it's the Leo in me), but just as often I'll go fully monochromatic. Not to be flashy, just because a head-to-toe single color, done right, looks completely put together.

I think a put-together closet matters too: recycling what you already own, changing it up with how you're feeling that day, and finding the pieces you can always reach for and mix and match, even on the days you're feeling lazy but still want to look pulled together.

HerStyleVault is still a small business, and part of growing it means continuing to build on what I already know. What I'm confident in is helping someone who feels stuck find their identity again through their clothes. Some days that looks like comfort, some days it's chic and minimal, some days it's a cute little dress that makes you feel good.

I want to help everyone feel comfortable in their body while still finding the courage to step outside their comfort zone — there's nothing that makes my heart warmer than seeing that shift happen.

Whatever it is, being put together is the thing I care about most, and it's what I want to help you feel every time you get dressed. My passion truly lies in sharing what I love and the feeling I get every time I put an outfit together — that's why I started this business, to share that with you, while feeling your best.
